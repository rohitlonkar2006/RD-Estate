Privacy Notice

This notice describes the current behavior of the RD Estate static website. It is not a substitute for the privacy terms of third-party services you choose to use.

Information entered in the inquiry form

The website does not submit form entries to an RD Estate server or save them in a website database. If you choose to submit the inquiry form, your browser opens WhatsApp with a draft containing the information you entered. The draft is sent to RD Estate only if you review and send it in WhatsApp. WhatsApp is operated by a third party and its own privacy terms apply.

Other third-party services

Some pages may load fonts, icons, maps, or media from external providers, and map or contact links may take you to third-party websites or apps. Those providers may receive technical information such as your IP address and browser details when your device requests their content. Their privacy policies govern that processing.

Cookies and analytics

This website does not intentionally use its own analytics service or store inquiry details in browser storage. Third-party services embedded or linked from the site may use cookies or similar technologies under their own policies.

Your choices

You can choose not to use the inquiry form, avoid opening third-party links, or contact RD Estate directly by phone or email instead. Do not include sensitive personal or financial information in an inquiry.
