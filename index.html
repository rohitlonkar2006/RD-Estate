What to Verify Before You Buy

Review project-specific records independently and confirm every detail before booking.

Ask for plot records

Request the title chain, land record extracts, approved layout and applicable permissions, encumbrance information, survey/demarcation details, and draft sale agreement for the project you are considering.

Check charges and terms

Get a written, itemized quote covering taxes, registration, infrastructure or maintenance charges, payment milestones, possession terms, and cancellation/refund conditions.

Use independent advice

Have a qualified local property lawyer and relevant authorities verify the documents and approvals. This website is not a substitute for legal or financial advice.

Our Land Plotting Process

Transparent, systematic approach to ensure you get the best plots with complete peace of mind.

1. Strategic Land Acquisition

Review the locality and access information published for each project, then confirm current availability with the team.

  • Compare project localities
  • Check plot sizes and listed rates
  • Ask for a dated cost sheet
  • Arrange a site visit

2. Thorough Legal Verification

Ask for plot-specific title and approval records, and have them independently reviewed before booking.

  • Title deed verification
  • Encumbrance certificate
  • RTC & mutation checks
  • Approval from relevant authorities

3. Visit and Confirm

Visit the site and confirm plot boundaries, access, infrastructure, charges, and terms in writing before proceeding.

  • Verify the plot location on-site
  • Confirm roads and utilities
  • Review the final cost and payment terms
  • Check agreement and possession details

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about our land plots and buying process.

Are the listed rates and plots guaranteed to be current?

No. Rates and availability can change. Ask the team for a dated written quote and current plot inventory before visiting or paying.

Can I see the legal and approval documents before booking?

Request documents for the specific project and plot. Have an independent property lawyer verify ownership, permissions, encumbrances, layout and agreement terms.

What is included in the listed price?

Confirm in writing whether taxes, registration, infrastructure, maintenance, and other charges are included. The rate shown on this site is not a complete cost estimate.

How do I arrange a site visit?

Use the inquiry form below to choose a project and preferred date. It opens a prefilled WhatsApp message; you review and send it from WhatsApp.

Can I compare projects or get a brochure?

Use the comparison checkboxes in the available plots section. A downloadable brochure is currently linked from the Gurudatta Park listing.
